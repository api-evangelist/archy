# Archy

> Archy is an all-in-one, cloud-based dental practice management platform that consolidates scheduling, clinical charting, imaging, insurance verification, billing, patient communication, payments, and reporting into a single system, with AI agents that automate routine practice workflows. Founded in 2021 (Jonathan Rat and Benjamin Kolin) and backed by TCV, Bessemer Venture Partners, CRV, and Entrée Capital ($47M total funding through a 2025 Series B). Archy handles protected health information and is HIPAA-compliant; it exposes no public developer API.
